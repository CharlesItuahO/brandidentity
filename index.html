01 / DO

Maintain Uncluttered Clearspace

Always preserve a minimum clearspace equal to 1.0X cap-height around all four sides of the primary mark.

02 / DO

Approved High-Contrast Monochrome

Use single-ink reversed alabaster lockups when printing on dark dyed stocks or anodized metal plates.

03 / DON'T

Never Stretch or Compress

Do not alter aspect ratios or distort geometric stroke weights to force the mark into narrow containers.

04 / DON'T

No Drop Shadows or Unapproved Glows

Depth must be achieved solely through physical debossing, foil stamping, or approved palette contrast.
